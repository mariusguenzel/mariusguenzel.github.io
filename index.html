I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at Wharton, working in behavioral and corporate finance.

My work combines quasi-experimental research designs with ML methods, including computer vision on facial images and LLMs on text.

Research Themes

How do managers decide, and what does it cost?
Sunk costs and escalating commitment, and what the job itself costs and why.
Selected work
In Too Deep: The Effect of Sunk Costs on Corporate Investment CEO Stress, Aging, and Death
How does personality shape economic outcomes?
Measuring traits, and what they predict about career outcomes and loan default.
Selected work
AI Personality Extraction from Faces: Labor Market Implications Facing Default?
Where do economic beliefs come from?
Mental models, valuation models, and long-run growth expectations.
Selected work
Mental Models and Financial Forecasts Valuation Models
